# Code of Conduct

SMX Framework is committed to providing a respectful and welcoming environment.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior:

- Be respectful.

- Be constructive.

- Help others.

- Accept feedback.

- Encourage collaboration.

---

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment

- Hate speech

- Personal attacks

- Trolling

- Spam

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Violations may result in warnings or removal from the project.

---

Based on the Contributor Covenant.
