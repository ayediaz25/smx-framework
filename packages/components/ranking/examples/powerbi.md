# Power BI Guidelines

Recommended Width

350–450 px

---

Recommended Height

Auto

---

Recommended Usage

- Top 5 Customers
- Top 5 Products
- Top 5 Regions
- Top 10 Sales
- Executive Reports

---

Best Practices

✔ Limit rankings to 5–10 items.

✔ Sort descending.

✔ Highlight first position.

✔ Use badges or icons for the top three.
