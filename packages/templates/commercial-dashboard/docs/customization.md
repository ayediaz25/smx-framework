# Customization Guide

## Theme

Supports all SMX themes.

Example:

- Purple
- Blue
- Green
- Dark

---

## KPIs

You can replace KPI cards with your own measures.

Recommended:

- Sales
- Orders
- Margin
- Customers
- Ticket Average

---

## Charts

Supported visualizations:

- Line Chart
- Column Chart
- Area Chart
- Donut Chart
- Funnel
- Map

---

## Ranking

Ranking can display:

- Products
- Customers
- Vendors
- Categories
- Regions

---

## Filters

Supports:

- Date
- Region
- Product
- Category
- Channel
- Salesperson

---

## Branding

Replace:

- Logo
- Primary Color
- Secondary Color
- Icons
